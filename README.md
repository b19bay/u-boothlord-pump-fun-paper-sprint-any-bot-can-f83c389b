A two-sleeve pump.fun paper-trading bot. The deep-curve dip sleeve buys coins deep in their bonding curve (virtual SOL at least 60) when they dip to 90% or less of their 500-slot rolling peak, then exits at +20% or -10%. The launch-snipe sleeve buys at the first trade with real reserves of at least 1 SOL in minimum size, purely as insurance for the 20-coin minimum bar. Everything is calibrated on the kit's own 24-hour practice dataset: the launch spike is uncatchable at 2-slot latency, so the bot trades levels, not spikes. Paper trading only, no real funds.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
